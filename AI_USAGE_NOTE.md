# AI Usage Note

AI was used as a development and reasoning aid during the implementation of the prototype.

I used AI assistance to help structure the React frontend, Express backend, UI flow, prompt design and debugging process.

The final product decisions were reviewed and adapted based on the assignment requirements. In particular, I chose to make experiment assumptions visible, require clarification for important missing parameters, and clearly label simulated research results rather than presenting them as real trading performance.

The AI-generated implementation was tested manually through the complete user flow and debugging was performed when runtime issues occurred.

AI was therefore used as a development assistant, while the product structure, decisions and final implementation were reviewed by me.
